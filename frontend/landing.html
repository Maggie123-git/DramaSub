DramaSub Features

Character-name locking · 角色名锁定

Before translating, DramaSub extracts each character's name, persona, and relationships — then locks the name so the protagonist is called the same thing in every scene.

Genre presets · 剧集类型预设

Pick romance, suspense, costume, urban, fantasy, or sci-fi. Dialogue tone, honorifics, and terminology locking adapt automatically.

Whole-season context · 全剧上下文

DramaSub reads the entire transcript before translating, so tone, style, and names stay coherent from the opening scene to the finale.

Dialogue tone & honorifics · 台词风格

Formality, endearments, and era-specific titles are handled per genre, so characters sound in character.

Editable subtitle cards

Review, edit, split, and merge every subtitle card before exporting to SRT, VTT, TXT, or JSON.

59 languages + RTL

Ship to English, Spanish, Arabic, Persian, Japanese, Korean, and 53 more — with full right-to-left support.
